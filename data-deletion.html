Data Deletion Request

Submit a request to delete personal data processed by NewToken or by businesses that use the NewToken platform.

For Meta App Users: If you connected to a business via WhatsApp, Instagram, or Messenger and that business uses the NewToken platform, you can request deletion of your data here. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion by email.

What data we delete

End-user data

If you interacted with a business via WhatsApp or Instagram that uses NewToken, we can delete: your phone number or Instagram ID from our records, message metadata associated with your identifier, and any profile or contact data stored in our system.

Account data

If you have a NewToken business account, deletion will remove: your account profile, API keys, configured automations, stored templates, analytics data, and associated payment information (subject to legal retention requirements).

Deletion process

1
Submit request
Complete and submit the form above. You will receive a confirmation email with a reference number.
2
Verification (within 72 hours)
Our team will verify your identity to ensure we delete the correct data and protect against unauthorized deletion requests.
3
Deletion (within 30 days)
Your data is permanently deleted from our active systems. Some data may be retained in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before being purged.
4
Confirmation
You will receive a final confirmation email when deletion is complete.

Exceptions to deletion

Certain data may be retained even after a deletion request where required by law or legitimate business interest, including: data required for ongoing legal proceedings, tax records as required by Brazilian law, fraud prevention records, and anonymized aggregate analytics that cannot be linked to an individual.
