Privacy Policy

How NewToken collects, uses, and protects data processed through our platform and Meta API integrations.

Effective date: January 1, 2026 Last updated: March 14, 2026 Version: 2.0
Summary for Meta Reviewers: NewToken Technology is a B2B automation platform. We access Meta Platform Data (WhatsApp Business API, Instagram Graph API) solely to provide automation and messaging services for our business customers. We do not sell, share, or repurpose any data collected via Meta's APIs. Users may request data deletion at any time at https://newtoken.win/data-deletion.html.

1. Company Information

This Privacy Policy applies to NewToken Technology ("NewToken," "we," "our," or "us"), the operator of the NewToken AI automation platform accessible at https://newtoken.win.

NewToken provides technology services that enable businesses ("Clients") to automate customer communication workflows using Meta's messaging platforms, including WhatsApp Business API, Instagram Graph API, and Messenger. This policy governs how NewToken handles data belonging to our Clients, their end-users, and visitors to our website.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account and Business Data

When Clients create an account on the NewToken platform, we collect:

  • Business name, contact name, email address, and phone number
  • Billing information (processed by a certified payment processor; not stored directly by NewToken)
  • Meta Business Manager ID and WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID provided through the Embedded Signup process
  • API keys, webhook configurations, and platform settings

2.2 Meta Platform Data

Through authorized integrations, NewToken may access the following categories of Meta Platform Data on behalf of our Clients:

  • WhatsApp Business API: Inbound and outbound message metadata (message IDs, timestamps, delivery statuses), phone number identifiers of end-users who initiate conversations, and message content necessary to route and respond to conversations as configured by the Client
  • Instagram Graph API: Direct Message identifiers, Instagram User IDs (scoped to the app), and interaction metadata for accounts that engage with Client business profiles
  • Meta Business API: Business profile information, WABA configurations, and message template statuses

Meta Platform Data is accessed only with the explicit authorization of our Clients through Meta's OAuth consent flow, and only to the extent necessary to provide the services our Clients have configured.

2.3 Usage and Technical Data

We automatically collect technical data when Clients use our platform, including: API request logs, error logs, performance metrics, IP addresses, browser type, and session identifiers. This data is used for platform security, debugging, and performance optimization.

2.4 Website Visitor Data

Visitors to newtoken.win may have their IP address and standard HTTP request metadata recorded in server access logs for security and diagnostic purposes. We do not use third-party tracking cookies for advertising.

3. How We Use Data

3.1 Service Delivery

The primary purpose of all data collection is to provide and operate the NewToken platform. Specifically, we use data to:

  • Route messages between Clients' configured automation workflows and Meta's APIs
  • Maintain authentication sessions with Meta's APIs on behalf of Clients
  • Execute automation rules, AI responses, and message delivery as configured by Clients
  • Generate analytics and reporting for Clients on their own messaging activity
  • Provide technical support and troubleshoot issues

3.2 Platform Improvement

We use aggregated, anonymized technical data to monitor platform health, improve reliability, and develop new features. We do not use individual Client data or Meta Platform Data for this purpose.

3.3 Legal Compliance

We may process and retain data as required by applicable law, regulatory obligations, or valid legal process.

4. Meta Platform Data — Specific Obligations

NewToken operates as a certified technology provider under Meta's Platform Terms. With respect to data obtained through Meta's APIs, NewToken commits to the following:

  • No resale: Meta Platform Data is never sold, licensed, or transferred to third parties for any purpose
  • No profiling: We do not use Meta Platform Data to build user profiles for advertising, marketing, or behavioral analytics beyond the services our Clients have configured
  • Minimal access: We request only the API permissions strictly necessary to provide the services our Clients have purchased
  • Data minimization: Message content is retained only during the active processing window required for delivery and automation. Content is not stored for longer than operationally necessary
  • User rights: NewToken supports the right of end-users to request deletion of their data. Deletion requests can be submitted at newtoken.win/data-deletion.html

5. Data Sharing

NewToken does not sell, rent, or trade any personal data. We may share data with the following categories of service providers strictly to operate our platform:

  • Cloud infrastructure providers (for hosting and data storage, under data processing agreements)
  • Payment processors (for billing, under PCI-DSS compliant agreements)
  • Monitoring and logging services (for platform reliability, with data minimization controls)

All sub-processors are bound by contractual data protection obligations at least as restrictive as this policy.

6. Data Retention

NewToken retains data for the minimum period necessary for the stated purpose:

  • Message metadata (delivery receipts, message IDs): up to 90 days, for debugging and analytics
  • Message content: deleted within 24 hours of confirmed delivery unless the Client explicitly configures longer retention
  • Account data: retained for the duration of the Client relationship plus 90 days after account closure
  • Audit logs: retained for 12 months for compliance purposes

Clients may request earlier deletion of their data or their end-users' data at any time by contacting privacy@newtoken.win.

7. Data Security

NewToken implements industry-standard security measures to protect all data. These include TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, access controls with the principle of least privilege, multi-factor authentication for administrative access, regular security audits and penetration testing, and a formal incident response program. For full security details, see our Security page.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights with respect to data we hold about you:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@newtoken.win or submit a request through our Data Deletion page. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

End-users who interacted with a business via WhatsApp or Instagram and wish to request deletion of their data should submit a request at newtoken.win/data-deletion.html.

9. Cookies

The NewToken website (newtoken.win) uses only essential session cookies required for the platform to function. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

10. International Data Transfers

NewToken is based in Brazil. Data may be processed in cloud infrastructure regions outside Brazil. All international transfers are conducted under appropriate legal mechanisms including Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

11. Children's Privacy

The NewToken platform is a B2B service intended exclusively for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated to Clients by email at least 30 days in advance. Continued use of the platform after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
