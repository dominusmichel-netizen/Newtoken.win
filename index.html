How it works

From integration to automation in minutes

NewToken sits between your business and the Meta ecosystem, handling authentication, message routing, and automation orchestration.

01

Connect via Embedded Signup

Business clients authenticate their Meta accounts using NewToken's Embedded Signup flow. NewToken securely receives the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID and manages access tokens automatically.

02

Configure automations

Define message workflows, AI response rules, routing logic, and trigger conditions from the NewToken dashboard or via API. Templates are approved and managed programmatically.

03

Receive events via webhooks

Incoming messages, delivery receipts, and status updates are delivered to your endpoint via authenticated webhooks in real time. NewToken handles signature validation and retry logic.

04

Analyze and optimize

Comprehensive analytics provide visibility into automation performance, message volumes, response rates, and customer engagement across all connected Meta channels.
